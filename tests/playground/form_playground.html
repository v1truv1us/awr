AWR Form Playground

This page exercises every form element AWR's Tier 1 TUI is supposed to handle. Tab moves focus, Shift-Tab moves it backward. In text fields, just type. On checkboxes / radios, press Space (or Enter) to toggle. On buttons, press Enter to activate.

Jump to: Text inputs · Choices · Textarea & select · Buttons · Full form (GET) · Full form (POST)

1. Text inputs

Each input below should accept typing once you Tab to it. The focused field is highlighted in reverse video, and the box fills with what you type.

4. Button kinds

These all show as [Label]. The submit button submits its parent form. The reset button is currently a no-op visually. The plain <button> with no JS handler does nothing — but should still focus and accept Enter without crashing.
